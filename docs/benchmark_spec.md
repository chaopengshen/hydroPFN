Generated by `scripts/benchmark_spec.py` from `hydropfn.data.protocol.BENCHMARKS`.

Metric throughout: **median per-basin NSE on raw discharge (mm/day)**, computed by dmg_dev's own `Metrics` class (vendored verbatim). 365 days of spin-up precede every scored period and are never scored.

### Notes per benchmark

- **`peijun_li` — Peijun Li Ensemble**: spatial generalization; train and test periods overlap, which is not a leak because the basins are disjoint
- **`feng2023` — Dapeng Feng 2023**: trained and tested in the SAME period, different basins
- **`leo10_531` — 10 years 531 Leo**: the test period PRECEDES the training period -- backwards in time, and deliberately so
- **`leo15_671` — 15 years 671 Leo**: all 671 CAMELS basins, not the 531 subset
- **`leo15_531` — 15 years 531 (legacy temporal)**: same periods as leo15_671 on the 531 subset

## Holdout types

| Type | What is held out | Folds |
|---|---|---|
| **PUB** | 10 random basin groups, leave-one-group-out. Held-out basins are scattered, so a query's geographic neighbours are mostly *training* basins — "an ungauged basin among gauged ones". | 10 |
| **PUR** | 7 contiguous regions, leave-one-region-out. The whole region is out, so neighbours are mostly other *held-out* basins the model never trained on. The harder transfer test. | 7 |
| **temporal** | No spatial holdout — every basin is trained on and scored. The periods separate train from test. | 1 |

## Model arms

| Arm | Model | Params | What it sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lstm` | Regional LSTM | 0.30M | Forcings + static attributes, one LSTM across all training basins. Causal. Cannot read another basin. The field's reference bar. |
| `unitA` | Unit A standalone (SiteEncoder) | 3.4M | Same encoder as PUBModel but with NO connector and no context. Bidirectional. The honest no-context arm. |
| `pubC` | PUBModel, concurrent context | 7.2M | SiteEncoder + cross-site connector. Reads K neighbouring gauges' discharge on the SAME days being predicted (lag 0). |
| `pubL<N>` | PUBModel, lagged context | 7.2M | As pubC, but the neighbours' records stop N days before the prediction day. The window keeps its full 512 d of history, so lag and history length are independent. N in {1,3,7,16,32,64,128}. |

**K** = number of neighbouring gauges the model reads at inference. `K=0` is the same information a regional LSTM has and is the row directly comparable to it; `K>0` adds neighbour discharge, which an LSTM structurally cannot use.


## Donor baselines (reported beside every K>0 row)

| Baseline | What it does |
|---|---|
| `nn_donor` | Copy the single nearest gauge's hydrograph. Flat in K. |
| `ctx_mean` | Unweighted mean of the K neighbours' discharge. |
| `idw` | Inverse-distance-weighted mean of the K neighbours (1/d^2). No training, no parameters. The bar K>0 must beat. |

## Caveats that belong with any quoted number

- **Single seed.** Treat margins under ~0.015 as ties.
- **No pretraining.** Every model is trained from random init, one per fold. `load_stefaland_trunk` is never called, while two of the three dmg_dev reference rows *are* pretrained arms.
- **Two asymmetries favour PUBModel** over the LSTM: it is a bidirectional smoother where the LSTM is causal, and it reads neighbours' discharge where the LSTM cannot.
- **PUR region labels are wrong** in the source CSV (the `huc` column disagrees with the station ids for 467 of 531 basins). The blocks are valid and disjoint; only the names lie. Call them R0–R6, not "Pacific Northwest".
- **The LSTM overfits with budget** (PUB 0.654 @50ep → 0.628 @200ep), while the transformer arms improve. Compare at matched epochs and say which.
