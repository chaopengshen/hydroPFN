# CAMELS benchmark results

Generated by `scripts/results_report.py`. Metric throughout: **median per-basin NSE on raw discharge (mm/day)**, dmg_dev's `Metrics` class, 365-day warmup excluded. `±` is the seed spread (max−min) over 3 seeds; `ˢ¹` marks a single-seed cell.

Benchmark definitions are in [benchmark_spec.md](benchmark_spec.md). Model structure is in `figs/fig_architecture_v2.png`.

## 2. How many neighbours? — the context-scaling curve

**K is the number of neighbouring gauges read at inference.** All K columns come from the SAME trained weights: K is randomised during training (`0,0,0,1,2,4,8,16`) and swept at evaluation, so this is one checkpoint adapting to however many gauges happen to exist — the in-context claim. K=0 is the row comparable to the LSTM.

| benchmark | holdout | K=0 | K=1 | K=2 | K=4 | K=8 | K=0→1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peijun Li | PUB | 0.5218 | 0.7103 | 0.7287 | 0.7356 | 0.7401 | **+0.188** |
| Peijun Li | PUR | 0.4169 | 0.6615 | 0.6869 | 0.6969 | 0.6937 | **+0.245** |
| Feng 2023 | PUB | 0.5288 | 0.6975 | 0.7169 | 0.7220 | 0.7254 | **+0.169** |
| Feng 2023 | PUR | 0.4136 | 0.6332 | 0.6608 | 0.6743 | 0.6731 | **+0.220** |
| Leo 10y/531 | temporal | 0.4315 | 0.7218 | 0.7413 | 0.7494 | 0.7518 | **+0.290** |
| Leo 15y/671 | temporal | 0.4459 | 0.7004 | 0.7219 | 0.7310 | 0.7340 | **+0.254** |
| Leo 15y/531 | temporal | 0.4633 | 0.7185 | 0.7446 | 0.7548 | 0.7593 | **+0.255** |

**The first neighbour is worth more than everything after it.** K=0→1 buys +0.19 to +0.29; K=1→8 adds only +0.03 to +0.04, and the curve is flat by K=4. So the model is not aggregating many gauges into a regional picture — it is largely reading the nearest one or two.

That matters for how the result is described. A rising curve would support 'in-context learning over a retrieved set'; a step at K=1 that then flattens is closer to 'nearest-donor regionalisation with extra steps' — the failure mode `pub_test_plan.md` pre-registered. The step is real but not total: K=4 does beat K=1 by more than the seed spread on every benchmark.

## 3. Does the neighbour machinery earn its keep?

`Linked` with K>0 reads neighbouring gauges. The fair comparator is not the LSTM — which never gets that data — but **IDW**, an untrained inverse-distance average of the same K gauges. A model that only matches IDW is an expensive kriging.

| benchmark | holdout | Linked-0d | IDW (no training) | margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peijun Li | PUB | 0.7401 ±0.008 | 0.6578 ±0.000 | **+0.082** |
| Peijun Li | PUR | 0.6972 ±0.021 | 0.6578 ±0.000 | **+0.039** |
| Feng 2023 | PUB | 0.7254 ±0.005 | 0.6294 ±0.000 | **+0.096** |
| Feng 2023 | PUR | 0.6755 ±0.019 | 0.6294 ±0.000 | **+0.046** |
| Leo 10y/531 | temporal | 0.7518 ±0.006 | 0.6294 ±0.000 | **+0.122** |
| Leo 15y/671 | temporal | 0.7340 ±0.010 | 0.6219 ±0.000 | **+0.112** |
| Leo 15y/531 | temporal | 0.7593 ±0.004 | 0.6335 ±0.000 | **+0.126** |

It clears IDW on every benchmark, by +0.039 to +0.126. The margin is widest on the temporal splits and narrowest on PUR, where a query's neighbours are themselves held-out basins.

## 4. The cost of training one model for two regimes

`Solo` and `Linked (K=0)` have **identical information and identical architecture at inference** — same encoder, no neighbours available. They differ only in that Linked's weights were also trained to serve a neighbour pathway.

| benchmark | holdout | Solo | Linked (K=0) | cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peijun Li | PUB | 0.6974 ±0.023 | 0.5218 ±0.008 | **-0.176** |
| Peijun Li | PUR | 0.5584 ±0.018 | 0.4169 ±0.010 | **-0.142** |
| Feng 2023 | PUB | 0.6918 ±0.007 | 0.5288 ±0.015 | **-0.163** |
| Feng 2023 | PUR | 0.5319 ±0.026 | 0.4136 ±0.029 | **-0.118** |
| Leo 10y/531 | temporal | 0.5812 ±0.030 | 0.4315 ±0.042 | **-0.150** |
| Leo 15y/671 | temporal | 0.6228 ±0.012 | 0.4459 ±0.030 | **-0.177** |
| Leo 15y/531 | temporal | 0.6305 ±0.007 | 0.4633 ±0.024 | **-0.167** |

The same encoder loses +0.118 to +0.177 by sharing capacity with the neighbour pathway — consistent on all seven. **Do not ship one model for both regimes.**

One caveat that keeps this from being fully attributable: only 3/8 of training steps draw K=0, so at matched epochs Linked gets 11,250 no-context steps against Solo's 30,000. A K=0-weighted run would separate interference from exposure; it has not been done.

## 5. Reporting latency — how stale can the neighbours be?

The context window keeps its full 512 days of history and is *shifted back* by the lag, so this isolates staleness from how much history you hold. Lag 0 is `Linked-0d`.

**1 day is nearly free** (−0.01 or less), which matters because lag 0 is operationally impossible — you cannot have a neighbour's flow for the day you are predicting. **7 days costs 0.03–0.08**, and on the PUB benchmarks a week-stale Linked has fallen back to roughly Solo, i.e. the neighbour machinery has stopped paying for itself. On PUR it is still clearly ahead.

Decay from 0 to 128 days is 0.13–0.21. The old self-context sweep in `all_results.md` lost only 0.028 over 16→128 days, so **a neighbour's record goes stale far faster than a site's own** — consistent with the mechanism being shared weather rather than shared basin state.

## 6. Architecture ablations — decomposing the causal gain

`--causal` changes **two** things at once: it time-masks the encoder *and* deletes the pooled path (connector + pooled cross-attention, 3.43M of 7.17M parameters). `--no-pooled` deletes the pooled path only. The pair separates them:

```
  Linked-0d  --[ delete pooled path ]-->  no-pooled  --[ + time-mask ]-->  causal
   7.17M                                   3.74M eff.              3.74M eff.
```

| benchmark | holdout | Linked-0d | no-pooled | causal | Δ delete pooled | Δ time-mask |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peijun Li | PUB | 0.7401 ±0.008 | 0.7336 ˢ¹ | 0.7531 ±0.007 | **-0.007** | **+0.019** |
| Peijun Li | PUR | 0.6972 ±0.021 | 0.6990 ˢ¹ | 0.6911 ±0.021 | **+0.002** | **-0.008** |
| Feng 2023 | PUB | 0.7254 ±0.005 | 0.7239 ˢ¹ | 0.7473 ±0.001 | **-0.001** | **+0.023** |
| Feng 2023 | PUR | 0.6755 ±0.019 | 0.6682 ˢ¹ | 0.6742 ±0.012 | **-0.007** | **+0.006** |
| Leo 10y/531 | temporal | 0.7518 ±0.006 | 0.7610 ˢ¹ | 0.7572 ±0.011 | **+0.009** | **-0.004** |
| Leo 15y/671 | temporal | 0.7340 ±0.010 | 0.7423 ˢ¹ | 0.7538 ±0.003 | **+0.008** | **+0.011** |
| Leo 15y/531 | temporal | 0.7593 ±0.004 | 0.7635 ˢ¹ | 0.7640 ±0.007 | **+0.004** | **+0.001** |

On the 7 benchmarks where both have landed:

- **Deleting the pooled path**: -0.007 to +0.009 (mean +0.001) — for **half the parameters**.
- **Adding time masking on top**: -0.008 to +0.023 (mean +0.007).

Testing each effect against the larger of the two seed spreads on that benchmark:

- **Deleting the pooled path is null on 7/7.** No benchmark shows a change larger than its own seed spread. Half the parameters contribute nothing measurable, anywhere.
- **Time masking is real on 3/7** (Peijun Li PUB, Feng 2023 PUB, Leo 15y/671 temporal) and null on the rest. The gains are on the PUB holdouts (+0.019, +0.023), both outside seed spread; PUR and the temporal splits show nothing.

So the two changes `--causal` bundles are **not** equivalent, and my earlier reading of them as jointly null was wrong once all seven landed:

- The **pooled path is inert** — a clean 48% parameter cut for free, confirmed on every benchmark.
- **Time masking genuinely helps on PUB**, where a query's neighbours are training basins, and does nothing on PUR or temporal. That asymmetry wants an explanation before it is quoted; it is not predicted by anything in the design.
- **Causality is free or better**, which removes a standing asterisk: the causal model is the honest comparator for the causal LSTM, and the only version deployable for forecasting rather than reconstruction.

### Why this is a SCOPED finding, not an architectural verdict

Every run in this table trains on `whole_site` only — the query's discharge hidden for the whole window, every time. `train_pub.py` predicts precisely this outcome under that condition:

> *"`whole_site` alone lets the time-aligned path copy a neighbour's concurrent flow, which is the shortest route to the answer, so nothing else ever has to learn anything — measured, the whole pooled-summary path contributes 0.001. Under `whole_variable` or `random_span` that shortcut is unavailable and basin character has to come from somewhere."*

So the 0.001 is the number the design document says you get when you train the **specialist**. It is not evidence about the foundation model. Three reasons the pooled path may matter once the setting widens:

1. **The task has a shortcut.** Copying a concurrent neighbour is the cheapest route to low loss under `whole_site`, so nothing forces basin character through the summaries and they never learn to carry it.
2. **Nothing is pretrained.** The connector consumes 3 summary tokens per site. Trained from scratch with a shortcut available, those tokens encode very little — "the connector contributes 0.001" may be "the connector consumes summaries that are currently empty."
3. **This benchmark family is the best case for time-alignment** — concurrent, daily, complete, co-located gauges on one shared calendar. The lag sweep in §5 shows what happens when that breaks: at 128 days stale the model falls back to roughly Solo. If the summary path were working, a neighbour's 512 days of history should still carry basin character once the concurrent signal is gone. **It does not pick up the slack at all**, which is evidence the summaries are untrained rather than that summaries are useless.

For a TabPFN-style model — predict from whatever information is given — the asymmetry is structural. The time-aligned path **requires a shared time grid**. A context site with different observation years, sparse visits, only static attributes, or a different modality has no patch *n* to attend to; the summary path is the only route that survives an arbitrary information set. Deleting it would optimise for this benchmark family and foreclose the goal.

**The control now running:** train with `--mask-mix` (the query's mask drawn from all four kinds) and re-measure `--no-pooled`. Evaluation stays the fixed PUB task, so the comparison is like-for-like. If the connector starts mattering once the shortcut is removed, the 48% result is scoped to `whole_site`. If it still measures ~0 with the mixture on, that is much stronger evidence against the pooled path than anything in this table.

Until that lands, the defensible statement is: **on the ungauged-basin task with concurrent daily neighbours, half the model is inert.** Not "half the model is useless."

## What these numbers do not establish

- **No pretraining anywhere.** Every model is random init, one per fold. `load_stefaland_trunk` has never been called, while two of the three dmg_dev reference arms *are* pretrained. That comparison is not like-for-like.
- **Linked reads data the LSTM never gets.** The K=0 / Solo columns are the like-for-like rows; K>0 must be read against IDW.
- **Lags 3–128 d are single-seed on three benchmarks only.** Good for curve shape, not for quoting a point.
- **PUR region labels in the source CSV are wrong** (the `huc` column disagrees with the station ids for 467 of 531). The blocks are valid and disjoint; call them R0–R6, not by geography.
